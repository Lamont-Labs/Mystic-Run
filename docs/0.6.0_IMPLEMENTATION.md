# Mystic Run 0.6.0 — Adventure implementation checkpoint

November 2, 2026 · Lamont Labs

Implemented: horizontal swipe basic combat without a basic attack button; tilt plus independent thumb-pad steering; separate always-available combat specials (Sunburst, Stone Pop, Starfall); earned contextual traversal abilities (Sunbreak, Skyglide, Riftwalk) at 60 character mastery XP; nine unique level/character treasure rooms; idempotent treasure collection; fourteen local achievements; mastery and next-goal presentation; treasure/badge galleries; privacy and confirmed local progress clearing; brighter architecture, floating lanterns, star pickups, bouncy feedback, expressive body/wing motion and enemy pop reactions.

Character selection stays locked during a run. Traversal abilities are optional for ordinary routes. Secret portals require the matching earned ability, lane alignment and an up swipe; the room lasts 96 metres and rejoins the ordinary encounter stream. Protected lessons award no mastery XP, including their practice sentinel. Unlocks, discoveries and deduplication survive profile serialization.

At this checkpoint: 112 adventure assertions, 148 native navigation assertions, 427 core, 38 control and 18 animation assertions passed. Tilt/elevation verification, long basic-only runs, rendered inspection, Google Play preparation and signed Android distribution are being completed before release. This checkpoint is not a claim of Google Play approval or final art approval.
