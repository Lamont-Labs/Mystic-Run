Mystic Run Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Mystic Run is developed by Lamont Labs. Questions may be sent to LamontLabs@proton.me.

Information handled by the game

This version works offline. It has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, in-app purchase or network permission. Lamont Labs does not receive your gameplay records through the game.

The game stores settings, run records, mastery, achievements, tutorial progress and treasure discoveries locally on your device. If you use tilt steering, motion-sensor readings are processed on the device to steer and calibrate the game. Sensor samples are not saved or transmitted. Optional vibration provides gameplay feedback.

Deleting local records

Use Settings → Privacy & Local Progress → Clear Local Progress to remove game records and their previous-save backup while keeping your control, audio and accessibility settings. You can also remove the app and its data through your device settings. This version disables Android app-data backup. There are no game accounts or server-side gameplay records to delete.

Contact and platform services

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to provide and use it to respond to your request. Google Play and your device operating system may handle their own platform data under their own privacy policies. This notice describes the game itself.

Changes

If the game's data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date.
