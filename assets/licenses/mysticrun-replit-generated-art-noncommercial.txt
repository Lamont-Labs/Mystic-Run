MysticRun Replit-generated artwork record

This record covers the three distant background illustrations generated with
Replit's image-generation tool and the source-conditioned rear-view character
action poses created with Replit's image-editing tool. Original generated PNGs
are preserved under art/premium-2d/source/.

The character poses were created from the owner-provided concept art and remain
subject to the owner's noncommercial MysticRun game-use permission. The
backgrounds were generated from text prompts without external reference images.
